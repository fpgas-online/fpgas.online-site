Key
  • # port number; Label the switch's own label for the port (its role in the title); LLDP the neighbour the port sees and the port it reports; MACs one address and how many more (all in the title); In / Out traffic; Err errors in / out.
  • VLAN the port's VLAN (+ other untagged; +Nt tagged); GW the gateway's interface for it (↑ up, ↓ down).
  • PoE: the watts drawn, else the state: on (power, no watts given), searching (PoE on, nothing drawing), off, fault, not known; no PoE on a port without it. ⏻ Power: off/on switches the board's PoE (board ports only).
