Key
  • operational: offered to visitors (it checks in, and its boot check of the boot it is running passed); needs attention: its check failed or could not finish, or it stopped checking in within 6 h; missing: not heard from for longer; checking: its check has not finished this boot.
  • State: ● operational, ! needs attention, ⟳ checking, ○ missing; the word and why on hover. The missing Pis are the second table.
  • Model, RAM, Serial: the Pi's label, a value a column (the label's words and the registered model in the Model's title); the serial links the Pi's own page. Port: the port the gateway last leased it an address on, the time in its title; empty when the gateway has no lease record (Notes then names the port its host name names). Board: the board's type; ID: the id on its label (the DNA, a USB serial), its name on hover; one board a line, – where its check read no id.
  • Boot check: the verdict of the check of the boot it is running; Checked: when that check reported. Offered (to visitors): ✓ links its visitor page, ✗ says why not on hover.
  • Up: the uptime it last reported. Seen: its last status beat. Times are {{ zone }}. The exact time is in each one's title.
  • Then a column per check across the fleet, grouped (basic, memory, peripherals, board specific) and in the order one needs another: ✓ ran and passed, ✗ ran and failed (the reason on hover; it links the Pi's page), ⊘ did not run because an earlier check did not pass, – not applicable to this board; a Pi with two boards has a mark per board, in the Board cell's order. Notes: what the page could not read (a revision, a clock, a lease).
  • Every column sorts: a click on its header, again to reverse, a third time for the served order (what needs a person first).
