Key
  • operational: offered to visitors (it checks in, and its boot check of the boot it is running passed); needs attention: its check failed or could not finish, or it stopped checking in within 6 h; missing: not heard from for longer; checking: its check has not finished this boot.
  • Model, Memory, Serial: the Pi's label, a value a column (the label's words and the registered model in the Model's title); the serial links the Pi's own page. Port: the port the gateway last leased it an address on, the time in its title; empty when the gateway has no lease record (Notes then names the port its host name names). Board: each board's label where its check read one, one a line.
  • Boot check: the verdict of the check of the boot it is running; Checked: when that check reported. Offered: "yes" links its visitor page; Why not says why a Pi is not offered.
  • Up: the uptime it last reported. Seen: its last status beat. Times are {{ zone }}. The exact time is in each one's title.
  • Then a column per check across the fleet: ✓ passed, ✗ failed (the reason on hover; it links the Pi's page), – not applicable (the board does not run it); a Pi with two boards has a mark per board, in the Board cell's order. Notes: what the page could not read (a revision, a clock, a lease).
