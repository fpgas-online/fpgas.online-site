Key
  • operational: offered to visitors (it checks in, and its boot check of the boot it is running passed); needs attention: its check failed or could not finish, or it stopped checking in within 6 h; missing: not heard from for longer; checking: its check has not finished this boot.
  • Pi: its label (model, memory, serial). Port: the port its host name says it booted on. Board: each board's label where its check read one.
  • Boot check: the verdict of the check of the boot it is running. Tests: a table for each board type, a row per board and a column per test it runs: ✓ passed, ✗ failed, – another result (skipped), blank when the board does not run it; the result is in the mark's title, the check's reasons in the Reason column.
  • Up: the uptime it last reported. Seen: its last status beat. Times are {{ zone }}. The Pi's host name and model are on its own page.
  • Offered: "yes" links its visitor page; Why not says why a Pi is not offered. Notes: what the page could not read (a revision, a clock).
